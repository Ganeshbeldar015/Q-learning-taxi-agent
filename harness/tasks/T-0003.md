# T-0003 — Hyperparameter Tuning & Final Multi-Seed Models
Backlog ref: REQ-005, REQ-006 · Sprint: 3 · Priority: MUST

## Intent
Execute a systematic hyperparameter search across learning rate ($\alpha$), discount factor ($\gamma$), and exploration decay schedule to discover parameters that maximize convergence speed. Save the winning configuration to `config/best.yaml`, and perform final multi-seed training (5 distinct seeds) to generate `results/q_table_final.npy`.

## Scope / Out of scope
- **Scope**:
  - `src/tune.py`: Stage A (one-factor-at-a-time) and Stage B (focused grid) search supporting parallel multi-worker execution.
  - Evaluation of configurations against selection criteria: $\ge 80\%$ convergence rate, final validation reward $\ge 7.0$, minimal mean episodes-to-convergence.
  - Export tuning trial metrics to `results/tuning_results.csv`.
  - Save winning configuration to `config/best.yaml`.
  - Retrain on 5 independent training seeds (distinct from validation and test seeds) saving `results/q_table_final.npy` and checkpoints in `results/final_runs/`.
  - Unit tests in `tests/test_tune.py`.
- **Out of scope**:
  - Evaluation on 1,000 unseen test seeds (offset 10000) and publication plots (deferred to `T-0004`).
  - Interactive Jupyter notebook and final technical report (deferred to `T-0004`).

## Guidance
- Training seeds during tuning must not overlap with validation seeds (5000–5049) or test seeds (10000–10999).
- Primary Q-table in Phase 5 must be selected purely by highest validation reward, never by test reward.
- Best configuration must measurably beat the baseline configuration (mean episodes-to-convergence < 2300).

## Requirements
- `REQ-005`: Hyperparameter search (Stage A single-factor, Stage B grid search) with multi-worker parallelism and `config/best.yaml`.
- `REQ-006`: Multi-seed final model training (5 distinct seeds) saving primary Q-table and seed checkpoints.

## Acceptance
- `AC-005-a`: Given Stage A sweeps and Stage B grid, tuning logs results to `results/tuning_results.csv` and outputs winning parameters to `config/best.yaml`.
- `AC-005-b`: The best configuration demonstrates lower mean episodes-to-convergence than the baseline configuration across 5 seeds.
- `AC-006-a`: Given `config/best.yaml`, 5 seeds are trained; the primary Q-table is saved to `results/q_table_final.npy` selected solely by validation score.

## Risks / Notes
- Parallel execution: use Python `multiprocessing` or `concurrent.futures.ProcessPoolExecutor` with safe entry points (`if __name__ == '__main__':`).
- Maintain determinism by explicitly seeding each worker trial with its configured seed.
