# T-0004 — Evaluation Benchmark, Visualizations, Notebook & Final Report
Backlog ref: REQ-007, REQ-008, REQ-009, REQ-010 · Sprint: 4 · Priority: MUST

## Intent
Deliver the final evaluation benchmarks, comparative policy table, publication visualizations (V1–V9), interactive Jupyter notebook, and formal technical report to complete the project deliverables.

## Scope / Out of scope
- **Scope**:
  - `src/evaluate.py`:
    - Evaluates policy greedily ($\epsilon=0$) over 1,000 unseen test episodes (seeds 10000–10999).
    - Logs mean reward, reward standard deviation, 95% bootstrap confidence interval, mean steps, success rate, and total illegal actions (`results/test_metrics.json`).
    - Produces comparative table contrasting: Random Policy, Untrained Q-learning Agent (random tie-breaking on zeros), Baseline Trained Model (`results/q_table_baseline.npy`), and Tuned Final Model (`results/q_table_final.npy`), saved to `results/comparison_table.csv`.
  - `src/plots.py`:
    - V1: Mean reward vs episode with standard deviation band across 5 seeds (`results/figures/V1_learning_curve.png`).
    - V2: Steps per episode curve with smoothing (`results/figures/V2_steps_per_episode.png`).
    - V3: Epsilon schedule curve showing decay rate and floor (`results/figures/V3_epsilon_schedule.png`).
    - V4: Validation reward curve with convergence threshold marker at +7.0 (`results/figures/V4_validation_curve.png`).
    - V5: Hyperparameter tuning sweeps / heatmaps (`results/figures/V5_tuning_heatmap.png`).
    - V6: Test reward distribution histogram (`results/figures/V6_test_reward_distribution.png`).
    - V7: Test steps distribution histogram (`results/figures/V7_test_steps_distribution.png`).
    - V8: 5x5 policy action map grid with arrows for key passenger/destination configurations (`results/figures/V8_policy_grid.png`).
    - V9: Animated replay GIF of a greedy test episode (`results/figures/V9_replay.gif`).
  - `notebooks/taxi_q_learning.ipynb`:
    - Self-contained interactive notebook executing end-to-end, loading models, generating live demonstrations, and displaying all visual plots and metrics.
  - `report/REPORT.md`:
    - Formal technical report detailing MDP formulation, Bellman equations, tuning ablation, empirical benchmarks, and analytical error bounds.
  - Tests in `tests/test_evaluate.py` and `tests/test_plots.py`.
- **Out of scope**:
  - Training additional models (all final models are stored in `results/`).

## Guidance
- Evaluation must strictly use seeds 10000–10999; never train or tune on test seeds.
- All numbers cited in `report/REPORT.md` and `README.md` must match saved files in `results/`.
- Target criteria: 100% success rate, $\ge 7.5$ mean reward, $\le 14$ mean steps, 0 illegal actions.
